#About Me
My name is Jakub Moos, I am a third year mechanical engineering student at UNC Charlotte. I wanted to study mechanical engineering because I love taking things apart and learning how they work. I'm driven by challenges and having to take the time to break them down and then solve them using analytical reasoning and practical experience. My interest in mechanical engineering comes firsthand from working on cars and understanding the principle behind a design. 

I keep learning more about myself as an engineer through my coursework. I am developing a mindset that is focused on problem solving and continuous improvement. The coursework I am taking has strengthened my foundations in math, thermodynamics, mechanics, and analysis. Additionally, hands on projects have helped me realize that being successful is not always finding an answer on paper and requires understanding how a design will behave in the real world and recognizing the constraints. I value the process of having to test an idea and learning from my mistakes in order to refine the final solution.

I want to become an engineer who can bridge the gap between theory and practical application. I want to continue developing skills in manufacturing, machining, 3D printing, and design. I hope to have an internship in the summer where I am able to put all the knowledge I have acquired into real life and gain more hands on experience. Ultimately, I see engineering as a combination of technical knowledge, creativity, and responsibility and I want to build a career where I can apply those qualities to meaningful problems, continue learning from experienced engineers, and develop solutions that are both technically sound and practical.
